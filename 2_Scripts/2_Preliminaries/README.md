# 2_Preliminaries

Descriptive and diagnostic analysis used to motivate the Brown pitch.

## Included Scripts

- `01_descriptive_statistics.do`: builds the sample-selection table for the hazard-panel estimation sample and records the first-award risk-set counts.
- `02_balance_checks.do`: reserved for pre-treatment balance checks around lottery exposure and the hazard risk set.
- `03_preliminary_figures.do`: reserved for maps, spatial clustering figures, and event-time descriptive plots.
- `04_specC_shock_diagnostics.do`: audits whether the Spec C 2023 estimation sample is mechanically tilted toward positive or negative recentered neighbor R1 shock, and summarizes that shock by expected-exposure tercile and locale.
