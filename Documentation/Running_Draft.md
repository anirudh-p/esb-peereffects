# Running Draft

## Working Question

Do local ESB subsidy shocks and adoption events affect nearby districts' subsequent ESB applications, awards, or adoption?

## Core Claim

The project estimates spatial spillovers from quasi-random nearby subsidy exposure, then uses timing and mechanism evidence to interpret whether the spillovers are more consistent with peer learning, supply/vendor push, or capacity constraints.

## Empirical Spine

1. Spatial fact: nearby ESB adoption/exposure predicts focal district behavior.
2. Lottery reduced form: nearby lottery shocks affect focal behavior.
3. Design-based IV: recentered/design-based exposure isolates plausibly random local exposure.
4. Timing and mechanisms: event-study and channel tests discipline interpretation.
